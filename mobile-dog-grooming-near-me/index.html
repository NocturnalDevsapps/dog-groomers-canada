What to confirm before booking mobile grooming

Service area and fees

Ask whether your address is inside the groomer's current route, whether there is a travel fee, and whether your postal code has a minimum package or waitlist.

Parking and setup

Confirm driveway, condo, street parking, power, water, winter access, and weather policies. Mobile vans may need level parking and enough space around the vehicle.

Dog fit and package

Share your dog's breed, size, coat condition, age, temperament, and last groom date. Ask what is included, what costs extra, and whether large or matted dogs need approval first.
