Editorial Policy

Dog Groomers Canada is built to help people compare grooming options with clear navigation, source transparency, and original booking guidance.

Directory data

Profiles use business listing facts such as name, city, address, phone, website, rating, hours, service signals, and map links when available. We do not publish customer review text.

Original guidance

City, service, profile, guide, and tool pages add practical comparison notes so visitors know what to confirm before booking a grooming appointment.

Corrections

Businesses and visitors can request updates, removals, or corrections by email. Changed details are reviewed before the static site is regenerated.

Editorial standards

Guide articles are written for Canadian dog owners who need practical grooming context before contacting a business. Articles focus on coat care, seasonal risks, breed needs, safety questions, appointment preparation, and what to confirm with a groomer or veterinarian.

We avoid presenting directory listings as endorsements. Ratings, maps, websites, photos, and service notes are treated as comparison signals, not guarantees. Visitors should confirm current details directly with the business before booking.

Review and updates

Static pages are regenerated when listing data or editorial content is updated. Correction requests should include the page URL, business name, city, requested change, and a source such as the business website or official social profile when available.

Advertising and independence

Listings are not endorsements and are not ranked because a business paid for placement. Advertising may appear on the site after review approval, but ads do not change directory facts, city pages, service pages, or correction handling.

To avoid misleading visitors during review, display ad placements are disabled until the site is approved and ready to serve ads.

Images and copyrighted material

Some business profiles display publicly available listing images when image data is present, and a site-owned illustrated placeholder is used when no image is available. Images are included to help visitors identify businesses and compare local options. Business owners can request image removal or correction by email.
