How to compare bath and brush dog grooming

Bath and brush visits can maintain skin and coat health between full grooms when the package matches the dog's coat.

Coat-specific shampoo

Ask about sensitive skin, shedding, odor, allergies, and whether conditioner or medicated products are appropriate.

Drying method

Confirm how the dog will be dried, especially for anxious dogs, seniors, puppies, or heavy-coated breeds.

Maintenance schedule

Ask how often your dog should return based on shedding, lifestyle, coat length, and brushing at home.
