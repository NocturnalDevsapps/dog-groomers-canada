How to compare dog de-matting

De-matting can be uncomfortable if a coat is tight, so safety and comfort should lead the decision.

Comfort first

Ask when shaving is safer than brushing out mats and whether severe matting requires multiple visits.

Skin checks

Mats can hide irritation or sores, so confirm how the groomer handles sensitive areas.

Prevention plan

Ask what brush, comb, and schedule will prevent the same matting from returning.
