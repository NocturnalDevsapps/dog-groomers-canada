How to compare puppy grooming

Puppy grooming is about building comfort as much as getting clean, so short, gentle appointments matter.

First-groom plan

Ask whether the appointment focuses on bath, brush, nails, face tidy, and positive handling instead of a long full groom.

Vaccination timing

Confirm age requirements, vaccine expectations, and whether the puppy should visit before the adult coat changes.

At-home prep

Ask what brushing, paw handling, and sound exposure will make future visits easier.
