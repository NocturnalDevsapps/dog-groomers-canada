How to compare dog teeth brushing

Non-veterinary teeth brushing or cosmetic cleaning is not a substitute for veterinary dental care.

Scope of service

Ask whether the service is brushing, breath care, cosmetic cleaning, or a referral to a veterinarian.

Health concerns

If gums bleed, teeth are loose, or pain is present, contact a veterinarian before grooming add-ons.

Maintenance

Confirm how often brushing is recommended and what at-home products are safe for dogs.
