How to compare dog nail trimming

Nail appointments are quick, but they still depend on the dog's comfort level, nail length, and whether grinding is available.

Trim or grind

Ask whether the business clips, grinds, or offers both, especially for thick or dark nails.

Handling needs

Mention fear, sensitivity, past quicking, senior mobility, or whether two handlers may be needed.

Walk-in rules

Confirm whether nail trims need an appointment, proof of vaccination, or a bundled grooming package.
