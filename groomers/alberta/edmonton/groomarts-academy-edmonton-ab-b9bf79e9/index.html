GroomArts Academy

Directory recordOfficial website facts checked 2026-07-28Business-submitted listing received 2026-07-27Photo permission documented

GroomArts Academy is a dog grooming school in southwest Edmonton that provides hands-on, salon-style training with instructors supervising students as they work with pets. Customers who choose a student groom receive 50% off the academy's regular grooming rates, giving pet owners a lower-cost option while students build practical experience. The published curriculum covers bathing, brushing, blow-drying, de-shedding, nail trimming, ear cleaning, sanitary care, clipping, scissoring, styling, safe handling, coat health, and breed-specific coat types. Contact GroomArts Academy to confirm current appointment availability, eligible services, the regular price before the discount, and how the groom will be supervised.

Questions worth asking this groomer

These questions are customized from the profile's location and service signals. They help you compare comfort, safety, price scope, and coat fit before booking.

  • Are you currently accepting new dogs in Edmonton, and how soon is your next full-groom appointment?
  • For my dog's size and coat, what is included in the quote from GroomArts Academy?
  • What add-ons or coat conditions could change the price after you see the dog?
  • How do you handle anxious dogs, senior dogs, puppies, or dogs that need breaks?
  • Can you keep the coat at the requested length, or would matting make a shorter haircut safer?
  • Are face, feet, sanitary trim, ears, nails, and finish work included in the haircut package?
  • Do you offer nail grinding as well as clipping, and is it priced separately?
  • Can you work with dogs that are nervous about feet or have thick or dark nails?
  • Which shampoo and conditioner would you use for this coat and skin condition?
