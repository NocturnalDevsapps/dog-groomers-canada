The Blissful Dog

Directory recordBusiness-submitted listing received 2026-10-30Photo permission documented

The Blissful Dog is a women-owned dog grooming salon at 6385 Main Street in Whitchurch-Stouffville, Ontario, run by co-owners and groomers Annelise and Caitlin. Its October 2026 submission confirms full grooms, bath-and-brush appointments and nail trims. The official website also lists bath-and-tidy visits, breed clips, puppy grooming, deshedding and a self-serve dog wash. Owners can book online through Groomore or call the salon to discuss their dog's coat, preferred finish and handling needs. The website describes appointments that begin with a coat check and a discussion of the requested haircut; it asks clients to mention nervousness, senior-dog needs or a first groom when booking. Published hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday, 9 AM to 3 PM; Sunday is closed. Confirm the package inclusions, appointment length and current quote directly. The seven salon and grooming photos were supplied by the business with permission to display them.

Questions worth asking this groomer

These questions are customized from the profile's location and service signals. They help you compare comfort, safety, price scope, and coat fit before booking.

  • Are you currently accepting new dogs in Whitchurch-Stouffville, and how soon is your next full-groom appointment?
  • For my dog's size and coat, what is included in the quote from The Blissful Dog?
  • What add-ons or coat conditions could change the price after you see the dog?
  • How do you handle anxious dogs, senior dogs, puppies, or dogs that need breaks?
  • Can you keep the coat at the requested length, or would matting make a shorter haircut safer?
  • Are face, feet, sanitary trim, ears, nails, and finish work included in the haircut package?
  • Do you offer nail grinding as well as clipping, and is it priced separately?
  • Can you work with dogs that are nervous about feet or have thick or dark nails?
  • Do you offer short puppy intro appointments before a full adult groom?
