Jurassic Bark provides dog grooming at Unit 106, 4342 West Shore Parkway in Langford, British Columbia. Its official grooming menu describes full grooms with bathing, drying, brush-out or de-shedding, nail trimming and ear cleaning, with haircut options and hand-stripping discussed for the dog's coat. Selected maintenance services are offered on a drop-in basis from 9 AM to 4 PM; nail grinding and face, feet or sanitary trims require an appointment. The website lists general opening hours of 7 AM to 7 PM daily, closed statutory holidays; confirm grooming availability separately. Call or email for a coat-specific quote. Questions worth asking this groomer

These questions are customized from the profile's location and service signals. They help you compare comfort, safety, price scope, and coat fit before booking.

  • Are you currently accepting new dogs in Langford, and how soon is your next full-groom appointment?
  • For my dog's size and coat, what is included in the quote from Jurassic Bark?
  • What add-ons or coat conditions could change the price after you see the dog?
  • How do you handle anxious dogs, senior dogs, puppies, or dogs that need breaks?
  • Can you keep the coat at the requested length, or would matting make a shorter haircut safer?
  • Are face, feet, sanitary trim, ears, nails, and finish work included in the haircut package?
  • Do you offer nail grinding as well as clipping, and is it priced separately?
  • Can you work with dogs that are nervous about feet or have thick or dark nails?
  • Which shampoo and conditioner would you use for this coat and skin condition?
