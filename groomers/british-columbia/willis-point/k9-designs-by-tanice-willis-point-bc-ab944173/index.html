K9 Designs by Tanice

Directory recordBusiness-submitted listing received 2026-09-06Photo permission documented

K9 Designs by Tanice offers private, one-to-one dog grooming in Willis Point, British Columbia. Tanice describes the setting as a quiet forest retreat, with no kennels and an unhurried approach to each visit. Her owner-supplied grooming portfolio includes full grooms, bathing and fluff-drying, haircut and styling examples, and a puppy introduction that allowed time for treats and play while getting familiar with grooming sounds and sensations. The accompanying photo stories emphasize taking breaks when a dog needs time to settle. Email Tanice directly to discuss your dog's coat, the grooming you need, appointment availability, current rates, and directions before visiting.

Questions worth asking this groomer

These questions are customized from the profile's location and service signals. They help you compare comfort, safety, price scope, and coat fit before booking.

  • Are you currently accepting new dogs in Willis Point, and how soon is your next full-groom appointment?
  • For my dog's size and coat, what is included in the quote from K9 Designs by Tanice?
  • What add-ons or coat conditions could change the price after you see the dog?
  • How do you handle anxious dogs, senior dogs, puppies, or dogs that need breaks?
  • Can you keep the coat at the requested length, or would matting make a shorter haircut safer?
  • Are face, feet, sanitary trim, ears, nails, and finish work included in the haircut package?
  • Do you offer short puppy intro appointments before a full adult groom?
  • What age and vaccination timing do you recommend for a first grooming visit?
  • Which shampoo and conditioner would you use for this coat and skin condition?
